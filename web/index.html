How It Works

Pipeline Overview

From raw images to measured 3D volumes in seven automated stages

Stage 1

Model Inference

VGGT-1B transformer predicts 3D point maps and camera parameters from multi-view images

Stage 2

PLY Export

Adaptive confidence filtering, background masking, and statistical outlier removal

Stage 3

Clean & Extract

RANSAC leveling, DBSCAN clustering, and ArUco-aware object segmentation

Stage 4

Reconstruction

Poisson surface reconstruction with adaptive depth and density filtering

Stage 5

Watertight Repair

PyMeshFix hole filling with Open3D fallback for fully closed meshes

Stage 6

Evaluation

38 multi-perspective screenshots at 3 elevation rings for visual QA

Stage 7

Volume

ArUco-calibrated real-world volume computation with auto-resolution tuning
